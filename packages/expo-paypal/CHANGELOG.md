## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- [`ab4905a`](https://github.com/hzblj/expo-paypal/commit/ab4905a348e24822559cf78a5a7f1e33de1d2e54) Thanks [@hzblj](https://github.com/hzblj)! - PayPal for Expo on the native PayPal SDK 3: PayPal Checkout with App Switch, Pay Later and PayPal Credit, vaulting a PayPal account or a card, card payments with 3D Secure, PayPal's branded payment buttons as native views, and device data for PayPal's risk checks. Every flow is one promise that resolves `approved` or `canceled`, a config plugin registers the return links on iOS and Android, and a flow the system interrupted by ending the app is restored when it opens again.
